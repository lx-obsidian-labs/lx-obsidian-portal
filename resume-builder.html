CV builder privacy

LX Obsidian Labs operates this tool. Editing, previews and exports run in your browser. If you turn on draft saving, the editor stores your CV in this browser until you clear it. The builder creates no account or job application.

If you request AI help, we send only the text and optional job requirements you enter in the AI fields to Cloudflare Workers AI. Remove names, contact details, identity numbers and confidential information before sending. LX stores no AI excerpts or suggestions. The service uses short-lived request counters to limit abuse.
