How AI uses your text

When you request a suggestion, LX sends relevant career text and any job requirements you entered to NVIDIA for processing. The contact fields are excluded. Keep names, ID numbers, contact details and other private information out of the text you send. LX uses short-lived request counters and does not store your CV or send it to the Careers inbox.
