How AI uses your text

When you request a suggestion, LX sends the text you approve to Cloudflare Workers AI or, when it is unavailable, to OpenRouter through an endpoint that declares zero data retention. You can remove any details in the review step. The contact fields are excluded. Keep names, ID numbers and other private information out of the text you send.
