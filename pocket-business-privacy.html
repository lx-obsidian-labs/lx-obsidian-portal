Privacy Policy

Version 1.0 · 18 November 2026 · LX Pocket Business for Android

1. Who we are and scope

LX Obsidian Labs, South Africa, develops LX Pocket Business, an Android business recordkeeping application. This policy covers the app, its advertising and related support. Contact support@lxobsidianportal.co.za for privacy requests.

2. Business information you enter

The app is designed for local business recordkeeping without a mandatory online account. Information you choose to enter may include business names, logos, contact and tax details, customer names, telephone numbers, email addresses, addresses, transaction amounts, descriptions, dates, outstanding debts, catalogue items, quotes, invoices and related records. These are used to maintain your ledger, calculate summaries, prepare documents and manage customer obligations. You should enter only information necessary for your business. We do not sell your business records or use them as advertising targeting inputs.

3. Advertising and device information

The free app is supported by advertising. Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK), when active, may automatically collect and share IP addresses (including approximate location inferred from them), device or account-related identifiers such as advertising ID and app set ID, app and ad interactions, and performance or diagnostic information. Google and participating advertising partners may process these data to deliver and measure advertising, perform analytics and prevent fraud. Advertising may be personalized where permitted and subject to applicable consent. Non-personalized advertising can still use technical information. Google describes its practices at Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from partner sites and apps.

4. Choices and permissions

Where required, advertising consent must be requested separately from acceptance of terms. You can manage advertising personalization and reset or delete your advertising ID through supported Android settings, and use in-app advertising privacy options where provided. Refusing optional personalization does not remove core recordkeeping functionality. Device permissions, if requested, are used for the feature you choose, such as selecting a logo or attachment or receiving reminders. You may manage permissions in Android settings.

5. Storage, backups and sharing

Business records are intended to be stored on your device rather than routinely uploaded to LX Obsidian Labs. Android backup or device transfer may copy eligible app data depending on the app version, operating system and your device settings. Files you export, print, share or back up may contain customer or financial information and may be stored by the destination you select, including cloud storage or messaging services. Those services have their own privacy practices. We do not operate a mandatory cloud account for the local-only edition. Do not send confidential records to support unless necessary and appropriately protected.

6. Support and website

If you contact us, we receive the information you provide, including contact details, messages and any attachments. We use it to respond, troubleshoot, protect our services and meet legal obligations. Our website hosting provider may process connection information such as IP addresses and request logs for delivery and security. External websites and ad destinations have separate policies.

7. Legal grounds and recipients

Under applicable law, including South Africa’s Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA), processing may be necessary to provide requested functionality, comply with legal duties, pursue legitimate interests such as security and support, or rely on consent where required. Recipients may include Google and its advertising partners, hosting and communications providers, recipients you select, and authorities where disclosure is legally required. Service providers may process information outside South Africa subject to applicable safeguards. Business users remain responsible for the lawful handling of their own customers’ information.

8. Security

Android application isolation and device security help protect local records. Google states that its Mobile Ads SDK encrypts collected data in transit using TLS. Use a secure screen lock, current software and protected backups. No system is completely secure; this policy does not promise that every local file is separately encrypted. We limit access to information supplied to support to legitimate operational needs and respond to security incidents in accordance with applicable law.

9. Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them using available app controls, clear app storage in Android settings or uninstall the app. Clearing storage or uninstalling may permanently erase records on the device; export necessary records first. Backups, shared documents and copies held by recipients must be deleted separately; restoring a backup may restore deleted records. Support correspondence is retained only as necessary to resolve requests, meet legal obligations or establish or defend claims, then deleted or de-identified. Google applies its own retention rules: Google data retention. Contact us to request deletion of information we hold. We may retain information where legally required and explain applicable exceptions.

10. Your privacy rights

Subject to applicable law, you may request access, correction or deletion of personal information we hold, object to processing, withdraw consent for future processing and lodge a complaint. Email our privacy contact with your request; we may verify identity proportionately. For customer records held by a business using the app, contact that business first. In South Africa you may contact the Information Regulator.

11. Children

The app is intended for adult business users and is not directed at children. If you believe a child has supplied personal information to us improperly, contact us.

12. Changes and contact

We will update this policy when data practices change and provide additional notice or consent where required. New cloud authentication, synchronization or AI processing must be separately disclosed before use. Privacy enquiries: support@lxobsidianportal.co.za. General contact: info@lxobsidianportal.co.za.
