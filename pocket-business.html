Frequently asked questions

Is it free?

The app is designed to be free and ad-supported.

Does it replace an accountant?

No. It is a recordkeeping tool, not a bank, payment service or professional accounting or tax adviser.

Can I use it offline?

Core recordkeeping is designed to work offline. Online services and advertising may need internet access.
