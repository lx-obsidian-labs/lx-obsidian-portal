Your contribution directly fuels the development of AI-powered tools, open-source software, and digital infrastructure that empowers African businesses to compete globally.

01

Support a specific project or contribute generally to our development efforts.

03
Investment / Partnership

Strategic investment or equity partnership for companies looking to grow with us.

What happens after you reach out

  1. You contact us with the option and the rough amount you have in mind.
  2. We reply to confirm the details and the payment options actually available — nothing is charged on this page.
  3. You complete the support in whatever way you agree with us. No automatic billing, no minimum.
  4. We follow up on where it went, and — with your permission — add you to our supporters list.

What backers get

  • Honest updates on what your support helped ship — directly and through our Insights posts.
  • An early look at new work when we have something worth previewing.
  • Optional recognition: your name or company listed publicly, or stay anonymous — your call.
  • A view of what's next through our public activity on Insights and GitHub. We don't run a formal public roadmap yet, but we'll happily share context on request.
  • Bigger sponsorships: any recognition or deliverables are scoped and agreed in writing first.

ASSIST

Offer Assistance

We believe in the power of community. Whether you're a developer, designer, marketer, or domain expert — there's a place for you in building the future of African tech.

01
Open Source Contributions

Contribute to our open-source projects on GitHub. Fix bugs, add features, or improve documentation.

02
Project Collaboration

Partner with us on client projects or internal products. Bring your expertise, we provide the platform.

03
Mentorship & Knowledge Sharing

Share your experience with our team and the broader African tech community through workshops or talks.

04
Internships & Graduate Programs

We offer structured internship programs for students and recent graduates looking to gain real-world experience.

What happens after you apply

  1. You send the form (or email us directly — details are on the contact page).
  2. We read it. If there's a fit or a next step, we reply — usually by email first, to talk it through.
  3. We agree scope and terms up front: paid, contract, volunteer or internship. Nothing starts until that's clear.
  4. You start on a real, defined piece of work with the team.

What contributors get

  • Public credit for your work where you want it — code, design, writing or talks.
  • Real shipping experience on live products, useful for portfolios and references.
  • An early look at what we're building, when there's something you can try.
  • Some collaborations are paid, some are volunteer or open-source — we agree which, and on what terms, before any work begins.

TRANSPARENCY

Where your support goes

Whether it's funding, time or feedback, support keeps three things moving. Here's what that means in practice — described honestly, without invented percentages.

01 — Product Engineering

Building and improving the products themselves: the AI platform, Synapse AI, Vista Cinema, marketplace tools and the internal systems behind them. Design, code, testing and iteration — this is the bulk of what we do.

02 — Infrastructure

Hosting, domains, model and inference credits, CI, and the security and monitoring that keep everything online. Infrastructure is unglamorous, but it's what keeps the free tiers and the uptime honest.

03 — Content & Open Source

Documentation, tutorials, Insights posts and the open-source work on GitHub. Sharing what we learn takes real time, and support helps pay for it.

A note on numbers: we don't publish a fixed percentage split — it shifts with whatever we're building that month. If you want to know exactly where a specific contribution went, ask us and we'll tell you what it covered.
