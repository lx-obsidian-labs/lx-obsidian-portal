School methodology

Every lesson has a purpose.

Explain

Start with conceptual illustrations, vocabulary and worked examples.

Practise

Follow guided labs, then solve a similar task independently.

Demonstrate

Submit practical work, tests, reflection and a short explanation.

Improve

Use rubric-based feedback, targeted revision and resubmission.

Academic standards

Quality built into the school.

Formal assessment and moderation

Outcomes-mapped assignments, standardised rubrics, assessment records, independent moderation and learner appeals. Designed for accreditation readiness, not represented as accredited.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Before you begin

Do I need coding experience?

No. The first book is guided orientation; later books progressively build independent skills.

Can I enrol now?

Not yet. Public previews are available. We have not announced a price or launch date.

Is this an accredited qualification?

No. Obsidian Learn is an independent practical learning school. Academic quality policies are being developed, but accreditation has not been obtained.

What will I need?

A computer, internet access, a modern browser and a free code editor. Any optional AI service costs will be explained before enrolment.

Interested in the programme?

Contact us about planned enrolment. This is not registration or payment.
