COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you begin.

Which course should I choose?

Choose coding to build software, business technology for office skills, AI literacy to use AI responsibly, entrepreneurship to launch a business, cybersecurity to protect systems or data analytics to interpret data.

Can I take more than one programme?

Yes, once enrolment opens. They are independent beginner programmes.

Do I need a computer?

A computer and internet connection are recommended for practical tasks. A mobile phone can be useful for reading previews.

Can I enrol now?

No. Paid enrolment is not open. Prices and dates have not been announced.

Is the school accredited?

No. Obsidian Learn is independent. Academic quality processes are being developed; no formal accreditation is claimed.
