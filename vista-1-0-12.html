Vista Watch 1.0.12
What's new & how to use it

Released 17 November 2026 · Android version code 19 · Mobile, tablet and Android TV / Google TV · Optional update.

A stability-focused release with improved playback recovery, more useful TV controls and clearer ways to get help. Playback is working in our latest maintainer device testing.

What changed

  • Playback and catalog recovery: clearer errors and retry paths, independent catalog fallback, and improved handling of playback sources. The aim is to recover from loading problems rather than leave you with an unexplained empty screen.
  • TV navigation and playback: refined remote focus and scrolling, plus a playback-sources panel with next-episode controls and access to help.
  • Help & Support: official contact options, installation/account/playback guidance and problem reports you can review before opening your email app. TV support includes a QR code to continue on your phone.
  • Login feedback: actionable error dialogs with recovery options instead of a bottom error banner.
  • Personalisation and presentation: expanded avatar choices, refreshed artwork and consistent Vista Watch identity.
  • One Android release: mobile and TV downloads contain the same signed app, with a launcher for each device type.

Playback depends on third-party providers and your connection. Successful device testing does not guarantee every title or source on every device. Native iOS downloads remain separate unsigned developer builds requiring Apple signing.

Quick guide

Install the update

Tap the release notification to open Vista and check for the update. Read the changelog, download, then approve the Android installation prompt. On mobile you can also use Settings → About Vista Watch → Check for updates. The official downloads page offers both mobile and TV links. Install over your existing app rather than uninstalling first.

Choose a source on TV

During playback, press Menu or hold OK to open Playback sources. Choose with your remote; press Back or select Return to video to close the panel. Try another listed source if the current provider is unavailable.

Play the next episode

In the TV playback panel, toggle Autoplay next or select Play next episode when available. Automatic next-episode playback uses confirmed direct-stream completion; embedded provider players may need the manual button.

Change your avatar on mobile

Open Settings → Account → Edit Avatar, then choose an avatar. The new choices are also available through profile personalisation.

Report a problem

Open Settings → Help & Support → Report a problem. Choose a category, describe what happened and review the report. Device details are optional. Opening your email app does not send anything automatically: review and send the email there. On TV, scan the support QR to continue on your phone.

Recover from a login error

Read the error dialog and follow the recovery action it offers. If your connection failed, reconnect and try again. For account questions, use Help & Support → Account assistance.

Find other controls

On mobile, Settings groups Playback, Audio & subtitles, Downloads, AI & voice, Notifications, and Data & storage. Provider players may expose their own controls; support varies by source and device. These settings do not imply every mobile feature is available on TV.

Need a hand?

Email support@lxobsidianportal.co.za with your app version, device model, title and what happened. Leave out passwords and payment details.
