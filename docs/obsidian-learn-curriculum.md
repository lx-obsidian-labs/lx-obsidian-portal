# Obsidian Learn — flagship programme design

Engineering and publishing: LX Obsidian Labs. Independent, non-accredited, pre-launch learning programme.

## Instructional design
Use constructive alignment: define measurable outcomes, select assessment evidence, then develop instruction. Use worked examples, fading scaffolds, retrieval practice, spaced review, formative feedback and mastery resubmission. Each book contains diagnostic quiz, learning outcomes, illustrated concepts, guided labs, independent assignments, marking rubrics, reflection and references. Video lesson plan: 6–12 minute focused segments, captions/transcript, code demonstrations, downloadable starter code and practice. Design for low bandwidth and accessibility. No certificate claims until policy is approved.

## Stage checkpoints
1. Guided Study Tracker demo and explanation; reconstruct one feature without copying.
2. Independently create a responsive accessible website from a brief.
3. Deploy a tested application with API/database and secure configuration.
4. Evaluate an AI prototype with documented failure cases and human oversight.

## Assessment
Assignments 40%, checkpoints 20%, knowledge checks 10%, capstone 30%. Equal weighting within assignment and checkpoint groups. Proposed pass: 70% overall, 60% in every assignment and checkpoint, 70% capstone, remediation of critical privacy/security/integrity issues. Rubric: understanding 15, correctness 35, design/code quality 20, tests/safety 20, documentation/reflection 10. Tailor descriptors to assignment. Capstone: architecture 15, application 25, AI evaluation 20, safety/accessibility 20, testing/deployment 10, defense/docs 10. Academic integrity: disclose AI, verify claims, do independent explain-back and live/recorded defense. Use synthetic data only. Rubrics are proposals pending pilot calibration.

## Delivery plan
26 weeks, 6–8 hours/week proposed. Books 1–4: 8 weeks; 5–10: 12 weeks; 11–13: 6 weeks. Checkpoint activities integrated into weeks. Product roadmap: private content storage; authentication; payment webhook verification; entitlements; student progress; assignments and feedback; consent and data minimisation; video hosting with captions; audit accessibility and security; pilot and launch. No public paid files or simulated checkout. Book 01 prepared, remaining 12 not yet written. No price set.
