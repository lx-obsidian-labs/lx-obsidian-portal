WHO THIS IS FOR

Start where you are.

Students, professionals and educators. No coding required.

Outcome: Use generative AI effectively and verify its output.

OUTCOMES-BASED CURRICULUM

What you will study.

Each sequential module has a measurable outcome and practical assignment. The final capstone integrates these skills.

01 AI fundamentals

Planned textbook and lecturer slides

Outcome: Distinguish generative AI, search and conventional software.

Assignment: Compare a search result and AI answer; explain limitations.

Evidence: completed artefact, verification or test evidence, reflection and attribution where applicable.

02 Prompt design

Planned textbook and lecturer slides

Outcome: Construct clear instructions and evaluate improvements.

Assignment: Create three progressively improved prompts and compare results.

Evidence: completed artefact, verification or test evidence, reflection and attribution where applicable.

03 Research and verification

Planned textbook and lecturer slides

Outcome: Verify factual claims against primary sources.

Assignment: Check five AI-generated claims using a source matrix.

Evidence: completed artefact, verification or test evidence, reflection and attribution where applicable.

04 Privacy and responsible use

Planned textbook and lecturer slides

Outcome: Identify unsafe disclosure, bias and misuse.

Assignment: Review a fictional workplace AI policy and recommend safeguards.

Evidence: completed artefact, verification or test evidence, reflection and attribution where applicable.

05 Practical AI workflows

Planned textbook and lecturer slides

Outcome: Design a repeatable human-reviewed workflow.

Assignment: Submit a workflow, before/after outputs and independent quality check.

Evidence: completed artefact, verification or test evidence, reflection and attribution where applicable.
