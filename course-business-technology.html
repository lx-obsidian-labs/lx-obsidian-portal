FREE SAMPLE / BUSINESS ANALYTICS

Revenue is not the same as cash.

Fictional business figures

Invoiced salesR6,000
Paid salesR4,000
Paid expensesR2,100

Assuming zero opening cash and no other transactions, how much cash remains?

Choose one answer

Worked Excel example

Type in column C, Amount in E and Status in F:

=SUMIF(C2:C9,"Sale",E2:E9)
=SUMIF(C2:C9,"Expense",E2:E9)
=SUMIFS(E2:E9,C2:C9,"Sale",F2:F9,"Paid")

Unpaid sales are R2,000. Sales less expenses is R3,900; cash surplus is R1,900. Formula separators depend on regional settings.
