Client work / Brand identity / Concept stage

Forest Training Centre.
One identity. A stronger presence.

Corporate identity and launch-signage concepts for a vocational training centre in Middelburg, South Africa. The objective: help people recognise the institution quickly, understand its broad training direction and make an enquiry.

Brand architectureOutdoor signageEntrance graphicsClient review concepts
01 / Visual system

Institutional heritage, with a modern edge.

The design system builds on the centre's existing crest and navy-and-gold identity, adding a measured lime accent for directional emphasis. The same structure carries across the main board, supporting sign and glass entrance graphics.

04 / Approach

Purpose before ornament.

Following feedback, the concepts prioritise umbrella programme wording instead of a fixed list of qualifications, accessible language, representative community imagery and larger telephone numbers. Individual-specific endorsements and unconfirmed partner marks were removed from outdoor concepts.

The work displayed here is prototype and mockup design. It should not be interpreted as completed fabrication or installation. Production-ready files, exact dimensions, accreditation text, photographs and print specifications remain subject to the final approval workflow.
