What testers do

Test real workflows

Install early builds and complete short assignments: recording money, managing customers, tracking debts, creating documents, working offline.

Report honestly

Tell us what broke, with screenshots and steps to reproduce. Constructive criticism is the most valuable thing you can send us.

Stay opted in

Remain enrolled in the Google Play testing track for your assigned period so we can verify fixes on real devices.

The 90-day programme

Days 1–14 · Onboard

Apply, consent, get added to the Google Play testing track, opt in and install. Complete your first assignment with fictional business records.

Weeks 3–6 · Weekly challenges

One focused challenge per week: invoices, debt tracking, offline mode, backup and restore, performance and accessibility.

Weeks 7–12 · Go deeper

Regression checks, longer assignments and recognition for outstanding contributions. Day 90: programme review and invites to future intakes.

Rewards are earned, never promised blindly

Rewards recognise verified testing work: completed assignments, reproducible bug reports and useful feedback. Depending on the intake and management approval, rewards may include early access to upcoming apps, eligible premium features for three months where available, gift vouchers, airtime or data, approved cash testing assignments, and public recognition. Specific amounts are confirmed per intake before you accept an assignment. Rewards are never given for reviews, ratings or install counts.

Who can join

  • You own or can regularly access an Android device running Android 8.0 or later.
  • You have an active Google Play account (the email you give us must be the one on the device).
  • You can test regularly and stay enrolled for your assigned testing period.
  • You give honest, constructive feedback and report problems with screenshots and reproduction steps.
  • You respect confidentiality and responsible disclosure, and use fictional business records while testing.

South African applicants are prioritised; international testers are welcome where device coverage helps. Community testing is voluntary. Paid testing assignments exist only as separately approved, contracted work — this is not a job offer.

Questions

Do I have to pay anything?

No. Joining and testing are free and voluntary.

Do I need to leave a review or rating?

No, and please don’t as part of the programme. Rewards are based only on testing work and feedback sent directly to us.

Is this employment?

No. Community testing is voluntary. Any paid assignment is separately approved and contracted before work starts.

What happens to my data?

We use your application details only to administer the programme, add your Google account to the testing track and contact you about testing. Test with fictional business records, never real customer or financial data. You can request access, correction or deletion at any time.

What if I can’t continue?

Tell us. We mark you inactive with no penalty, and you can rejoin a later intake.
