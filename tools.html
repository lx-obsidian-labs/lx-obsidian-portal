How to use these free tools

For VAT-exclusive prices, multiply the amount by the VAT rate divided by 100, then add the tax to the original amount. For VAT-inclusive prices, divide the total by one plus the rate expressed as a decimal. At a 15% rate, R1,000 excluding VAT becomes R1,150 including VAT. Confirm the applicable rate for your transaction.

For a percentage discount, multiply the original price by the discount rate divided by 100 and subtract the result. A 10% discount on R1,000 gives R900. The text statistics tool counts words and characters for assignments, reports and drafts; reading time is an estimate.

For exchange rates, check the reference date and remember that banks may charge different rates and fees. Use LX PDF Studio for document tasks and Obsidian Learn for structured learning.
