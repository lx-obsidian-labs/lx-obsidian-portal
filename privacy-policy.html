Vista Cinema / Vista Watch

Privacy Policy

How Vista handles viewing preferences, recommendations and information shared with connected services.

Policy information for version 1.0.9 · Updated 29 October 2026

Important: Vista uses locally stored viewing preferences to help personalise the experience. AI features use an external NVIDIA NIM service, so information included in an AI request may be processed outside your device. Local storage does not mean all app activity stays offline.

1. Who operates Vista?

Vista Cinema (also referred to as Vista Watch) is an application by LX Obsidian Labs. For questions about this policy or privacy requests, contact vilanenathan@gmail.com.

2. Information used by the app

Depending on the features you use, Vista may handle information such as selected titles, viewing activity, favourites, recommendation preferences, app settings and messages you choose to send to the AI assistant. If you sign in, account-related information may also be handled by the authentication service used by the app.

The app's preference-learning feature is designed to keep its viewing profile on the device. You should not assume that account data, streaming activity or AI prompts are stored only on the device.

3. AI recommendations and age-aware features

Vista uses viewing patterns to personalise recommendations and may use them to adjust content suggestions for age appropriateness. Such inferences are estimates, not verified ages. They should not be treated as a reliable substitute for parental controls or adult supervision.

When you use cloud AI features, relevant prompts or context may be transmitted to NVIDIA NIM infrastructure to generate responses. Do not include passwords, financial details or other sensitive personal information in AI conversations.

4. External services

Some functions depend on third-party services, which may process technical information needed to provide their functionality. These can include AI inference, movie and television metadata, authentication, video sources and software downloads. Their own privacy policies may apply. The precise information shared depends on the feature and provider in use.

5. Storage and security

Locally stored app preferences remain on your device unless a feature explicitly synchronises or sends relevant information to an external service. Removing app data or uninstalling the app may erase local preferences. Information previously sent to external services may be subject to those providers' retention rules.

We aim to use reasonable safeguards, but no online service or device storage method can be guaranteed completely secure.

6. Your choices

  • You can choose whether to use optional AI features.
  • You can clear locally stored app information using applicable device or app settings; doing so may reset recommendations.
  • You can contact us to ask questions about information associated with your use of Vista.
  • Parents and guardians should supervise children's use of the app; inferred age is not identity verification.

7. Children

Vista's recommendation safeguards are not a guarantee that all content will be suitable for children. A dedicated children's experience may be developed separately. Parents and guardians remain responsible for assessing suitability and supervising use.

8. Privacy rights and lawful processing

We aim to process personal information only for legitimate, disclosed purposes and in accordance with applicable law, including South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) where applicable. Subject to legal requirements, you may request access to, correction of or deletion of personal information we hold about you, object to certain processing, or raise a concern with the Information Regulator. We may need to verify your identity before acting on a request and may retain information where the law permits or requires it.

Contact us using the address below. This policy does not limit rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

9. Third-party content and service availability

Vista may display metadata, links, media or results supplied by independent third parties. Such material is provided by its respective owners or providers, and its availability, accuracy, legality and suitability may change. References to a film, service or provider do not imply that LX Obsidian Labs owns, licenses or endorses that content. Users must comply with applicable law and any third-party terms. We may remove or restrict access to features or references where necessary to address legal, safety or operational concerns.

10. AI outputs and age estimation

AI-generated responses, recommendations and inferred age categories may be incomplete, inaccurate or inappropriate. They are informational and should not be relied on as verified facts, professional advice, age verification or guaranteed child-safety controls. Parents and guardians should use appropriate device-level safeguards and supervise minors. We may adjust, suspend or disable AI features where required for safety, compliance or service reliability.

11. Security, interruptions and limits

We take reasonable steps appropriate to the information and systems under our control, but cannot promise uninterrupted availability, error-free AI responses or absolute security. Network operators, device manufacturers and independent service providers operate systems outside our direct control. To the extent permitted by applicable law, LX Obsidian Labs does not warrant the accuracy or continuous availability of independent third-party content or services. Nothing here excludes liability that cannot lawfully be excluded, including liability arising from our own unlawful processing of personal information.

12. User responsibilities

Users should keep their devices and accounts secure, avoid entering sensitive information into AI chats, use lawful content sources, and assess whether recommendations are appropriate for their circumstances. You must not misuse the app, interfere with its security or use it to infringe the rights of others. These responsibilities do not reduce the company's obligations under applicable law.

13. Updates to this policy

We may update this page when app functionality or service providers change. The revised policy will be published at this URL with an updated date.

14. Contact

For privacy questions, email vilanenathan@gmail.com or visit our contact page.
